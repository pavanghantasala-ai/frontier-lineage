# Paid Time Off Policy

## Annual PTO
Employees in their first 2 years of service accrue 15 days of PTO per year. Employees with 3 to 5 years of service accrue 20 days of PTO per year. Employees with 6 or more years of service accrue 25 days of PTO per year.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on March 31.

## Holidays and sick leave
Meridian observes 10 paid holidays each year. Sick leave is separate from PTO, and employees receive 8 sick days per year.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
