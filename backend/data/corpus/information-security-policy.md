# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Periodic password rotation is not required, but multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every system.

## Phishing
Suspected phishing emails must be reported with the Report Phish button in Outlook within 1 hour of receipt. Do not forward suspected phishing emails to colleagues.

## Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to the IT Service Desk at extension 4357 within 4 hours.

## Data handling
Customer data is classified as Restricted. Restricted data must never be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts. USB storage devices are blocked on all company laptops.
