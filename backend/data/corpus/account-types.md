# Consumer Account Types

## Everyday Checking
Everyday Checking has a $10 monthly maintenance fee. The fee is waived with a $1,500 minimum daily balance or $500 in monthly direct deposits.

## Premier Checking
Premier Checking has a $25 monthly maintenance fee, waived with $15,000 in combined balances. Premier Checking includes no ATM fees worldwide and a dedicated relationship banker.

## High-Yield Savings
High-Yield Savings allows 6 free withdrawals per month. Each withdrawal beyond 6 costs $5.

## Overdrafts
The overdraft fee is $30 per item, with a maximum of 3 overdraft fees per day. No overdraft fee is charged when the account is overdrawn by $50 or less.
