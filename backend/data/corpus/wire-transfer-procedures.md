# Wire Transfer Procedures

## Cutoff times
The cutoff for same-day domestic wires is 4:00 pm Eastern Time. The cutoff for same-day international wires is 2:00 pm Eastern Time. Wires received after the cutoff are processed the next business day.

## Fees
Outgoing domestic wires cost $25. Outgoing international wires cost $45. Incoming domestic wires cost $15, and incoming international wires cost $20.

## Verification and limits
A callback verification is required for any wire over $10,000 sent to a new beneficiary. The daily online wire limit is $50,000 for personal accounts and $250,000 for business accounts.
