# Card Disputes and Replacement

## Filing a dispute
Customers must dispute a card transaction within 60 days of the statement date. Provisional credit is issued within 10 business days of the dispute being filed.

## Investigation timelines
Most dispute investigations are completed within 45 days. Investigations for international transactions or accounts open less than 30 days can take up to 90 days.

## Lost or stolen cards
A lost or stolen card is locked immediately in the Meridian app. Standard card replacement is free and arrives in 7 to 10 business days. Expedited replacement arrives in 2 business days and costs $15, which is waived for Premier Checking customers.
