# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week after completing their 90-day probation period. Branch staff and vault operations roles are not eligible for remote work.

## Core hours
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 am to 3:00 pm in the employee's local time zone. Remote employees must be reachable on Teams during core hours.

## Equipment and security
Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500. All remote access must go through the MeridianConnect VPN. Printing customer documents at home is prohibited.

## Working abroad
International remote work is limited to 14 days per calendar year and requires HR approval at least 3 weeks in advance.
