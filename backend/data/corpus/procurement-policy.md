# Procurement Policy

## Purchase thresholds
Purchases under $2,500 can be made with a corporate card. Purchases between $2,500 and $25,000 require three competitive quotes. Purchases over $25,000 require a formal RFP and approval from the Procurement Committee.

## New vendors
Every new vendor must pass a vendor security review before handling Restricted data. The vendor security review takes about 15 business days.

## Contracts
Contracts longer than 3 years require review by the Legal department.
