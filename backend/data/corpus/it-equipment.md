# IT Equipment Standards

## Laptops
New hires receive a laptop on their first day and can choose a MacBook Pro or a ThinkPad X1. Laptops are refreshed every 4 years.

## Accessories
Employees can expense a second monitor up to $300. Headsets for call center staff are provided by IT.

## Software
Software not on the approved catalog requires a request in the ServiceNow portal and a security review.
