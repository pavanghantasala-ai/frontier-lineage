# Travel and Expense Policy

## Meals
The domestic per diem for meals is $75 per day. The international per diem for meals is $110 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an approved client dinner.

## Lodging
The standard hotel cap is $250 per night. In New York City and San Francisco the hotel cap is $325 per night. Bookings must be made through the Concur travel portal.

## Air travel
Employees fly economy class by default. Premium economy is allowed when a single flight segment is longer than 6 hours. Business class requires written approval from an Executive Vice President.

## Expense reports
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the trip ending. Itemized receipts are required for any expense over $25. Reports under $5,000 are approved by the employee's manager, and reports over $5,000 also need Vice President approval.
